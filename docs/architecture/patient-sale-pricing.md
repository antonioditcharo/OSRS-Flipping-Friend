# Patient sale pricing

Current changes: [durable plans and timely maintenance](trade-plan-maintenance.md) supersede the
same-second exclusion, mixed-plan erasure and full-horizon routine target veto described below.
The retained original contract explains earlier behavior; current delivery comes from HANDOFF.

19 October 2026. The owner reports premature break-even/scrap-profit exits. Read HANDOFF for the
installed versus queued pair; availability of a Sell card does not validate its pricing quality.

Verified stock is listed promptly, without discounting a healthy target to maximize an unvalidated
fill model's profit/time ratio. During the configured holding horizon, an available original target
is a minimum limit alongside the exact taxed acquisition-cost floor. A higher current sell-side
quote is retained. No target is invented for old trades. An initial sale without target context
uses the current sell-side limit and exact cost floor. Quiet-market initial/relist guidance follows
the same target protection and retains explicit trade ages/no fill promise; quiet prices never
authorize loss cuts or cancellation of a working sell.

Routine maintenance is shared by the client, final companion authority and replay. It requires:

- The full predicted sell time (or existing leg-horizon fallback), at least one checking interval.
  New entry plans retain sell-leg duration instead of the whole round trip; legacy context is not
  rewritten or reinterpreted as an exact fill time.
- No observed counter progress during the existing stale-offer interval (at least eight minutes
  and at least the configured check interval). Heartbeats do not move this clock.
- Fresh two-sided trades and two consecutive completed recent five-minute high-price buckets
  with actual buy-side volume supporting a materially lower ask. One spot print, future/incomplete
  bars, missing volume, contradictory duplicate bars or stale support cannot justify cancellation.
- A meaningful reduction after price stabilization, preserving the original target within the
  holding horizon and the exact taxed subset-cost floor. An unplanned healthy managed listing
  keeps its existing ask until that horizon; unknown-cost personal sales keep money unknown.

The holding horizon uses TradingHorizon.maxHoldMinutes, including custom holds and the actual
5m/30m/2h/8h attendance floor. After expiry, supported stalled-offer repricing may move to break-even;
elapsed time alone cannot authorize a loss or a modeled discount below the current sell-side
limit on an initial/relist order. Fresh stops and the player's explicit Finish session
(sell only) request bypass ordinary patience/target protection. Maintenance retains its existing
exact non-loss floor unless a fresh stop authorizes a cut; initial wind-down exits retain their
existing loss permission. Its tooltip explains faster exits can sacrifice profit or take a loss.
The setting is never silently changed. Existing price/source/physical/ACK/account
fences still decide whether any order is actionable. Withheld maintenance leaves the working offer
in place and allows unrelated guidance; it does not become an account-wide buy pause.

The local tracker persists an additive observed-fill clock and copies it to workers. The companion
independently joins the latest durable disposal for the exact current offer to its authenticated
source, validating account/session/episode/slot/counters/price/ordering within the sale read
transaction. Missing or contradictory progress is unknown, never evidence that an offer stalled.
No database schema migration, historical replay, cost repair, money reset or new execution claim.
Initial/relist price stabilization and final authority recheck the target as well as the exact floor.
The paired sale protocol is companion-sale-3; old semantics cannot authorize the new client's order.
Original live context now comes from [canonical acquisition evidence](acquisition-exit-context.md),
not an expiring item plan or a later client snapshot. Missing legacy context stays unknown.
If final authority declines a routine reduction, the client withdraws it and continues through the
independently verified entry path. This is never permission to bypass entry cash/slots/risk/limits;
urgent cuts and unlisted-stock refusals keep their priority recovery WAIT. The diagnostic remains.

Replay version 5 uses the same rules and retains the selected entry's target/stop/sell duration
through partial fills and simulated restart. Older bundles require their retained evaluator.
Remaining replacement/relist/foreground coverage gates stay open. This policy is an explicit
patience/profit-preservation choice, not calibrated optimal pricing or measured superior returns.
Real learning remains HOLD; financial valuations and entry-risk reserves do not use target prices.

Acceptance: a fresh player-collected healthy buy gets a Sell card at its retained target or higher
market limit; a young or recently filling sale is left in place; mature supported reductions retain
the target; only expired supported exits/stops/explicit wind-down may sacrifice it. Manually cancel,
collect and relist one appropriate mature offer, verify remaining quantity/profit and restart.
The agent reads source/ledger/authority/timing evidence directly; all game actions remain manual.
