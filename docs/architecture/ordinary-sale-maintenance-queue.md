21 October 2026. Actual delivery: `local-data/ordinary-sale-queue-20261021/delivery-state.json`.

The client previously queued relists, stock exits and listed stops through final companion authority,
but ordinary maintenance returned its first proposal without retaining alternatives. A refused first
reduction therefore hid later supported reductions until another planning pass chose a different item.

Once an ordinary sale wins the existing maintenance ordering, retain all later eligible sales in an
immutable, pass-local queue. Try each exact proposal through existing final authority, withdrawing
every rejected foreground/editor instruction. Only the actually presented authorized episode may
commit its durable cancellation/relist promise. Late fills and collection still cap its exact remainder;
an offer still occupying its slot is not treated as collected. Missing evidence retains the offer.

The first eligible buy keeps its previous priority when it precedes every eligible sale. Once a sale
wins, collecting its alternatives skips later buys, avoiding unseen buy deadline/replacement mutations.
Fresh stops, collection, ready exits and relists retain their existing priority. Price selection,
market patience/support, exact taxed subset floors, original plans, risk, accounting and policy versions
are unchanged. Replay already checks later sale maintenance after final refusal; this closes a client
selection gap without claiming complete production replay parity.

Behavioral regressions cover real engine selection → final authority → client presentation → durable
cancel/late fill/collect/restart remainder, exact subset costs, stale prices/recent fills, floor refusal,
account changes/slot reuse, buy priority and fresh-stop priority. Existing delivery tests retain
generation/revision withdrawal and all-refused behavior. Native controlled trading acceptance,
