# Distinct continuation successors

Prospective workflow association preserves parallel same-item cancel/collect promises. Placing one
replacement consumes only its proven origin; another origin retains its own cap and accepted sale
reduction. This is operational allocation, never FIFO ownership, accounting or placement authority.

## Capture and durability

Accepted replacement/relist advice carries an explicit original continuation identity and exact
account, side, item, price and quantity. The client commits an immutable acceptance UUID and
client-thread order before showing actionable advice. Failed prospective writes cannot self-commit
on a later callback. A changed foreground or failed save withdraws successor attribution independently
of ordinary learning history. Forecasts keep their own display-acceptance clock.

The first authenticated exact zero-fill offer capture freezes version-1 successor evidence in the
forced account outbox. Same-second order must be prospective within the same process. Later advice,
changed inputs, already-filled first observations and reused acceptances cannot backfill a binding.
The outbox retains both qualified and unbound first-source receipts through ACK, EMPTY and restart.
Existing source IDs, sequence/session keys, prediction identities and financial rows are unchanged.

The tracker checkpoints before synchronous source capture. New episodes without durable first-source
classification therefore preserve an UNKNOWN barrier and withhold affected guidance; they cannot
destroy a sibling plan before capture. The post-capture checkpoint resolves exact association or
retains conservative unknown-order supersession. A missing source remains pending even after the
offer disappears. A restarted/cached board never supplies missing collection or counter facts.

## Quantity and authority

Each direct successor reserves its exact placed quantity once from its immediate origin. A smaller
order leaves an explicit residual; cancelling a child does not refund its parent. The child's own
accepted maintenance retains only its observed returned remainder. Grandchildren cannot consume or
replenish the root twice. Consumed origins remain evidence for peer exemptions and late original
counter contradictions. Overcommit withholds the affected item, retaining the evidence.

The editor preserves an explicit foreground origin across its checking overlay and quantity edits.
Without an explicit origin, only a unique eligible saved continuation may bind; ambiguous same-item
hints cannot fall through to generic pricing. Exact canonical subset cost and personal-extra guards
still apply. Original acquisition contexts remain separate from workflow allocation.

Companion sale policy 7 independently qualifies canonical first sources chronologically against the
original sale, cancellation, collection and residual budget. Only proven successors gain peer
exemption. Replay 16 carries prospective displayed acceptance and uses the same core claim reducer.
Fresh market support, exact taxed floors, original stops, CURRENT_ACCOUNT_V3, funds, source, exposure,
loss, slot and limit guards remain mandatory. No historical plan/disposal repair or learning promotion.
