## Acquisition evidence

ordering proof. An exact zero-fill placement may retain its original exit plan even when advice
and placement share a recorded second. Refreshed/later advice, another process, mismatched orders
and already-filled first captures cannot establish that original plan. Prediction comparability
remains separate; legacy source/learning keys and financial history are not rewritten.

The canonical pool retains each acquisition origin's target, stop and sell estimate through partial
fills and replacements. Average-cost disposals cannot identify which origin remains: the ledger
therefore carries possible remaining quantity ranges. Exact pooled/subset costs remain the sole
accounting basis. Initial exits protect the applicable original targets; automatic loss authority
requires positive proven stops for every possibly remaining origin and fresh evidence crossing all
of them. Missing context never creates a stop from current risk settings.

The old three-bludgeon original plan is unavailable in retained source/outbox evidence and stays
unknown. The bolts' separately recorded original and replacement plans can be projected without

Cancellation permission follows the exact accepted episode through late fills, collection and
capped relisting. Final companion authority rechecks the original source chain, returned quantity,
current market and exact cost. A cancelled offer cannot silently regain the old target or supply
permission to another offer. Missing or changed evidence retains the promise and withdraws guidance.

Buy maintenance distinguishes age, actual progress and original forecast deadlines. Heartbeats and
refreshes do not extend those clocks. Deadline persistence has its own account-scoped forced-atomic

must materially improve the standing buy and use a completed, recent low-side bucket with actual
volume. Exhausted limits bypass ordinary patience. An overdue stalled remainder can be abandoned without an identical rebuy promise.
