## Completing an accepted routine reduction

Once a player has cancelled and collected a sale, missing optional bucket history must not require
them to earn the same reduction again. Shared maintenance version 7 may finish the saved routine
limit when the exact accepted receipt is available and the ordinary decision lacks bucket support.
Current complete prices must still pass the existing freshness checks. The limit is at least the
receipt's proposed price and the exact taxed remaining-cost floor, and must remain below the old
standing ask. It does not restore the original target or reduce the saved price further. It makes
no claim that the limit will fill promptly.

Only known-cost managed stock can use this fallback. Original clocks, receipt quantities, current
unlisted/inventory caps, account source lineage, canonical receipt issuance/acceptance and the
cancel/collect chain still qualify independently. A stop or wind-down receipt cannot authorize a
loss through the routine fallback. Fresh confirmed stops retain their existing separate path.
Missing legacy acceptance, stale prices, ambiguous sources or impossible non-loss floors remain
refusals. No financial rows, original acquisition contexts or risk anchors change. Storage and wire
formats remain compatible with #168. Chronological replay version 23 identifies the changed
continuation behavior; its synthetic cancelled/collected/restarted workflow retains the saved limit
without inventing a fill. Rollback may withhold a new fallback quote without altering
