## Canonical replacement requests

A collected BUY remainder supplies only its item, exact quantity cap and continuation origin.
With the companion active, the client must not suppress that request using a second spot-age,
low-plus-one cash or local buy-limit calculation. The canonical replacement planner independently
chooses and validates the actual order. Account identity, pending-source ACKs, final timing,
funding, exposure, limits, preferences and durable successor acceptance remain required.
The request marker has no price and never reaches the player without final authorization.
Zero available cash still permits evaluation of the proven acquired partial exit before reserving
the last slot. This is not a local price-dependent estimate of a canonical order's affordability.

## Refused sale quotes

Client and companion market/history polls are independent. A different modeled exit price is
not permission to relax final authority or repeatedly present an unsupported quote.
An additive version-1 `salePriceRevision` may accompany only a scoped price-refusal WAIT.
It contains a proposed price and exact canonical subset profit (or explicit unknown cost).
It is not authority, not a durable acceptance, and never changes the requested loss-cut mode.

The client validates account, order identity, source, policy, type and clocks, then retries once
with the revised price. The second request must pass all ordinary exact-order checks; maintenance
also requires its new matching durable receipt. No third request or quote-only fallback exists.
Source/quantity/account changes, missing receipts and repeated refusals still withdraw advice.
The original continuation origin persists; old-price forecasts are not copied to the new order.
Sale policy 9, existing receipt/proof formats and historical financial rows stay unchanged.

## Independent working maintenance

After every ready exit refuses, an urgent/unlisted refusal may still check other working offers,
excluding already refused episode identities. At most eight distinct episodes are considered.
Each requires its own final sale or BUY-maintenance authority and observation-generation fence.
The unresolved urgent exit still prevents a new-entry fallback. A successful urgent exit retains
priority; no original stop, cost, source or holding is manufactured to obtain it.
