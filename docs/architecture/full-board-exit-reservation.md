# Full-board churn and retained exit priority

23 October 2026. Current delivery is in HANDOFF and the private full-board-churn receipt.

The owner observed repeated cancellation of new buys, collection, and another buy at the same price,
plus alternating BUY/keep-the-slot-free guidance. A cancelled seven-unit Fury sale remained in
inventory. Frozen sources include zero-fill cancellations seconds after placement, below ordinary
repricing patience. The installed source reproduces three behavioral failures with synthetic data.

An engine refresh now takes one immutable ledger read for its sale, continuation and reservation
decisions. A concurrent heartbeat may invalidate the shared cache without making different helpers
in that same pass disagree about the holding. Missing initial evidence remains missing. This read
cannot authorize orders: final source/account/stock/cost/policy checks and delivery generation fences
remain independent. The context ends with the refresh; another refresh must obtain its own evidence.

The last-slot reservation survives refused routine maintenance and cancelled-sale pricing outages
at the client fallback boundary. An exact retained collected sale in current inventory keeps that
priority even while ledger reads retry. When current evidence returns, relisting still runs before
the reservation WAIT; it does not turn into a permanent veto of the intended sale.

Canonical planning, final entry and capped replacement also reserve the last slot for independently
proven acquired, unlisted inventory. Personal extras, bank-only/unseen stock, paused historical items,
fully listed quantities and active healthy accumulation cannot create this reservation. Two free
slots permit unrelated entries. An exact own-item capped replacement retains its separate path;
another ready holding still needs its exit slot. Every original funding, risk, limit and source guard
continues to apply. Moving stock out of observed inventory removes only this routing reservation,
not the financial holding, cost or exposure.

With no free slot, a proposed managed sale must pass current exact sale authority before the engine
asks the player to cancel a working buy to make room. Refusal leaves that buy working. A successful
preflight is not later placement authority: collection and the subsequent sale require fresh checks.
Ordinary supported repricing, proven stops, actual exhausted buy limits and sell-only remain separate.

Regressions use real engine/worker/client delivery and disposable durable continuation/account stores:
read invalidation mid-pass, repeated fallback attempts, outage/recovery, refused/accepted sale
preflight, collection to sale, spare capacity, canonical stale-plan refusal and reservation release.
Existing continuation, exact-cost, stop, restart and transport tests remain required. No financial
history, acceptance identity, original plan, unknown cost, gap or risk anchor is rewritten. Controlled
player-loop acceptance, sustained timing, complete replay and validated learning remain open.
