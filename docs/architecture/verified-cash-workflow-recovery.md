# Verified cash, stock estimates and durable workflows

19 October 2026. The owner reported long stock-valuation waits, "Partly unpriced", lost plans and
stranded managed trades, then explicitly chose verified-cash buys while accepting uncertain held
stock resale value. [Verification and delivery receipt](../history/verified-cash-workflow-recovery-2026-10-19.md).

## Cash is not a resale estimate

CURRENT_ACCOUNT_V3 retains the original durable risk window, exact ledger costs, tax/profit bounds
and qualified V2 risk marks. Price-only uncertainty for physically proven acquired units remains
reported as full unpriced cost, but is excluded from the account-wide loss charge. Those items are
paused for new entries, including cached/final/replacement decisions. Current cash still caps actual
spending; acquisition cost is not cash or liquidation value. Unsupported physical quantities retain
their full exact-cost charge. Unknown live offer exposure, unbounded scope, contradictory funding,
confirmed losses, stale source/feed, malformed/future prices, overflow, slots and limits still refuse.
No financial row, loss anchor, historical gap or original acquisition target is changed. V1/V2
snapshots remain readable under their original semantics.

Performance now reports verified cash and canonical purchase cost separately from display-only
last-quoted, after-tax stock estimates. The latter use observed quantities capped by canonical
remainders, retain the actual quote clocks and never feed risk, prices, accounting or learning.
Missing quotes/locations do not erase cash/cost; the known priced component remains visible with
a star and exact unpriced cost. Holding details show recorded/observed/priced quantities, purchase
cost and trade ages. Personal extras are excluded; a quoted estimate is not executable value.

## No account-wide lock from one continuation

Ready unrelated exits precede a buy replacement. A refused replacement or relist remains retained
while other exits and freshly authorized unrelated entries are checked; it cannot become a permanent
WAIT at the front of the decision chain. The same accumulation's partial fills remain protected.

Account-scoped version-2 continuation documents hold multiple original episodes rather than one
overwriting slot. Collection progress, separate managed-cost requirements and exact late-fill
remainders survive slot reuse/restart. New buy hints have no clock-only expiry. Retained well-formed
legacy buy hints keep their original identity/quantity but lose the old advice expiry because every
resulting order must be planned and authorized fresh. Deleted hints are never reconstructed from
financial history. Completion, a proven replacement, contradictory evidence and explicit player
rejection still end the relevant hint; rejecting one item does not discard the others. Unreadable,
foreign or future-format documents are preserved rather than overwritten as empty.

## Refresh and foreground boundaries

Old lifecycle captures cannot use a restarted worker. Rejected submissions release refresh ownership;
stale discarded results request a fresh capture. Final foreground delivery rejects expired/foreign
advice; the guide also withdraws expired displayed/pending authority. A queued player key cannot fill
an expired instruction, even if identical fresh advice arrives later. Notifications follow the
guide's actual accepted instruction. Game confirmation, placement, cancellation and collection
remain manual. Durable outbox ordering/identities/ACKs and accounting transactions are unchanged.

Replay policy `account-replay-9` carries separate persisted continuation queues, the V3 risk fence,
retained hints and exit-before-replacement/refusal behavior through restart. Older bundles require
their retained evaluator. These are behavioral/synthetic checks, not measured return or learning
promotion. Controlled full-loop/pricing quality, sustained timings and qualified 100M members/eight
slot 5m/30m/2h/8h benchmarks remain separate open gates.
